# tremor
Tremor: every earthquake of the past day, echoing across a dotted map of the Earth, wider and redder the stronger it was.

Press Begin or the space bar to replay the day, and space again to pause. Switch the span to a week, turn on a low knock for each quake, drag the map to turn it, and point at an ember to read it. Data from the [USGS](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/), map from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), plate boundaries from [PB2002](https://github.com/fraxen/tectonicplates). `tools/make-map.py` rebuilds `map.js`.
